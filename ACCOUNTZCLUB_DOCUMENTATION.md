# Accountz Club: Complete Platform Documentation

## 1. Welcome to Accountz Club
Accountz Club is a professional website made for buying and selling digital accounts and assets. It is designed to be very easy to use, secure, and fast. This guide explains every part of the website so you can understand how it works and what it can do for your business.

---

## 2. Who is this for?
The platform has three main groups of people using it:
1. **Buyers**: People who want to buy digital accounts, gaming assets, or streaming services.
2. **Vendors (Sellers)**: People who have accounts to sell and want a safe place to find customers.
3. **Admins (Staff)**: People who manage the whole website, help with problems, and keep everything running smoothly.

---

## 3. The Buyer Experience (Buying Stuff)

### 3.1 Marketplace & Finding Items
*   **Search Bar**: Type what you are looking for (like "Netflix" or "Zoom").
*   **Categories**: Browse groups like Social Media, Gaming, Streaming, or Trading.
*   **View Modes**: You can see items in a large Grid box, a simple List, or a tight Table view. 
*   **Balance Badges**: Added special badges that show you if an account has money already inside it (like a $50 credit). You can hover over these to see exactly what "Balance" means.

### 3.2 Your Tools & Pages
*   **Orders**: A list of every account you have bought. You can click on any order to see the login details or find help.
*   **Messages**: A private chat area where you can talk to sellers about their products or your purchases.
*   **Wishlist**: A "Save for Later" section. If you find something you like but aren't ready to buy, you can save it here.
*   **My Disputes**: If an account you bought is not working, you can open a case here. The website staff will look at it and help fix the problem.
*   **My Reviews**: See all the feedback and star ratings you have given to different sellers.
*   **Refund Requests**: A place to track your money if you asked for a refund because an account was broken.
*   **Billing & History**: Shows every payment you have made on the website so you can keep track of your spending.
*   **Settings**: Update your profile, change your email, or set a new password.
*   **Support**: If you have a question about the website, you can send a message to the staff here.
*   **Account Recovery**: A simple "Forgot Password" tool that lets you get back into your account using your email.

---

## 4. The Vendor Experience (Selling Stuff)

### 4.1 Managing Your Business
*   **Vendor Dashboard**: A main screen that shows your total sales, active orders, and how your business is growing.
*   **Adding Products**: You can add items one by one or use the **Bulk Upload** tool to add hundreds of accounts at once using a single file.
*   **Reports & Analytics**: Easy-to-read charts that show which days you made the most money and which products are popular.

### 4.2 Your Tools & Pages
*   **Orders**: A complete list of everyone who bought from you. You can see which orders were delivered and which are still active.
*   **Messages**: Talk directly to your buyers. This helps you provide great service and answer their questions.
*   **Refund Requests**: Manage requests from customers who want their money back. You can approve or talk to them first.
*   **My Disputes**: If a buyer reports a problem, it shows up here. You can explain your side and provide proof to the staff.
*   **My Reviews**: Read what your customers are saying about you. Good reviews help you sell more items!
*   **Billing & Payouts**: This is where you manage your earnings. You can see your balance and request to send your money to your own crypto wallet.
*   **Settings**: Set up your store name, write a bio about yourself, and add your payment addresses for getting paid.
*   **Support**: A direct line to the website admins if you need help with your seller account or have technical issues.

## 5. The Admin Central (The Control Center)

The Admin Panel is the absolute control center of the website. It allows staff to manage every single thing happening on the platform.

### 5.1 Main Management Pages
*   **Admin Dashboard**: A global overview of the entire website. It shows total revenue, new users, and any urgent alerts that need attention.
*   **Users Management**: Search, edit, block, or delete any buyer or vendor. You can also manually reset passwords for users here.
*   **Vendor Management**: A dedicated page to approve new sellers, check their performance, and manage their "Verified" or "Premium" status.
*   **Listings & Products**: View every product on the marketplace. Admins can approve new products, hide bad ones, or edit details if needed.
*   **Order Management**: See every single sale happening across the platform in real-time.

### 5.2 Financial & System Tools
*   **Crypto & Payments**: Manage the website's wallet, check transaction histories, and ensure all payments are being tracked correctly.
*   **Payouts Management**: Review and approve "Withdrawal" requests from sellers who want to get their money.
*   **Commissions & Earnings**: Set the platform fee (percentage) that the website takes from every sale and view total profit.
*   **Gift Cards**: Create and manage gift card codes for promotions or customer rewards.

### 5.3 Safety, Support & Content
*   **Disputes & Refunds**: The main hub for resolving issues. Admins can read chat logs between buyers and sellers and decide who gets the money.
*   **Tickets & Support**: Respond to support requests from all users. 
*   **Roles & Security**: Manage staff permissions. You can give different levels of access to moderators or other admins.
*   **Branding & Settings**: Change the website name, logo, colors, and legal pages (like Terms of Service) without touching any code.
*   **Categories & Tags**: Organize the marketplace by adding, editing, or removing product categories.
*   **Notifications & Alerts**: Send sitewide announcements or specific alerts to groups of users.

## 6. Safety and Trust

### 6.1 The Escrow System (Money Protection)
This is the most important part of the website:
*   When a buyer pays for an item, the money does **not** go directly to the seller. 
*   The website holds the money safely in "Escrow." 
*   Only after the buyer gets the account and says "Everything is okay," the money is given to the seller. 
*   If there is a problem, the money can be refunded to the buyer.

### 6.2 Verification Tiers
To help buyers trust sellers, the website has three levels:
*   **Verified**: Trusted sellers who have been checked by staff.
*   **Premium**: Top-level sellers with a clinical history and great reviews.
*   **Basic**: New sellers just starting out.

---

## 7. Operational Workflow (How a Sale Happens)
1.  **Listing**: The Seller puts an account for sale with a balance of $100.
2.  **Buying**: The Buyer finds the account and pays with Bitcoin or Monero.
3.  **Escrow**: The money is held safely by the website.
4.  **Delivery**: The Buyer gets the login details instantly.
5.  **Release**: The Buyer tests the account, clicks "Complete," and the Seller gets their money.
