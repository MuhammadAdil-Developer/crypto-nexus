# AccountzClub - User Guide & Payment Documentation

### Direct Payment (No-Escrow)
*   **How it works:**
    1.  You send funds directly to the **Vendor's personal wallet**.
    2.  **AccountzClub does not hold the funds.**
    3.  Once confirmed on the blockchain, the product is released instantly.
*   **Risks & Refunds (Important):**
    *   **No Automatic Refunds:** Since the vendor has the money, the platform *cannot* force a refund. The vendor must manually agree to send funds back to you.
    *   **Disputes:** You can still open a dispute if issues arise. If the vendor is found guilty of scamming, **their account will be banned**, but the platform cannot guarantee financial recovery of your funds.

### Escrow Payment (Recommended - 100% Safe)
*   **How it works:**
    1.  Your payment is sent to a **Secure Link Wallet (Escrow)** managed by AccountzClub.
    2.  The Vendor sees the order is "Secured" but **does not** receive the money yet.
    3.  The Vendor delivers the product.
    4.  You verify the product works.
    5.  **Release:** You verify everything is good and click "Release Funds". The money then moves to the vendor.
*   **Refund Protection:**
    *   If the product is bad, you open a dispute.
    *   Since the money is still in the Safe Wallet, **Admins can force a full refund** back to you immediately, regardless of what the vendor says.

## 3. Step-by-Step Payment Guide

### Step 1: Browse & Purchase
1.  Navigate to the **Marketplace** or specific category (e.g., Streaming, Software).
2.  Click on a product to view details.
3.  Click the **"Buy Now"** button.

### Step 2: Generate Invoice
1.  A "Secure Checkout" modal will appear.
2.  **Order Summary:** Review your item price.
3.  **Payment Method:** Select **Bitcoin (BTC)** or **Monero (XMR)**.
4.  **Payment Type:** Choose **"Direct Payment"** or **"Secure Escrow"**.
5.  Click **"Proceed to Payment"**.
6.  The system will generate a unique **Payment Address** and the exact **Crypto Amount** you need to send.

### Step 3: Making the Payment (Testnet / Live)
To complete the order, you need to send the exact amount of crypto to the displayed address.

> **Note for Testing Mode:** Since we are currently running on the Bitcoin Testnet network, you do not need real money. You can use free "faucets" to pay for your order.

**Instructions to Pay:**
1.  **Copy the Address:** Click the copy icon next to the "Payment Address" in the checkout window.
2.  **Copy the Amount:** Click the copy icon next to the "Total Amount".
3.  **Send Funds:** Go to one of the following websites (Testnet Faucets):
    *   [Bitcoin Testnet Faucet 1](https://bitcoinfaucet.uo1.net/send.php)
    *   [Bitcoin Testnet Faucet 2](https://coinfaucet.eu/en/btc-testnet/)
4.  **Paste Details:**
    *   Paste the **Payment Address** you copied into the "Bitcoin Address" field on the faucet site.
    *   Enter the amount (or a slightly higher amount to cover fees) in the amount field.
    *   Click "Send Bitcoins" or "Get Bitcoins".

### Step 4: Confirmation
1.  Return to the AccountzClub browser tab.
2.  The system constantly monitors the blockchain.
3.  Once the transaction is broadcasted (usually within 1-5 minutes), the payment modal will update to **"Payment Detected"**.
4.  The order status will change from **Pending** to **Processing** or **Paid**.

## 4. Receiving Your Product
Once your payment is confirmed:

1.  Go to your **Dashboard** > **My Orders**.
2.  Find the order you just paid for.
3.  **Status: Delivered/Processing:**
    *   If the product is an auto-delivery item (like a code or account), you will see a **"View Credentials"** button or "View Details".
    *   Click it to reveal the Email/Username and Password for your purchase.
    *   You can also download these credentials as a text file.

## 5. Refund and Dispute System
We have a robust system to protect your funds if something goes wrong.

### When to use it?
*   The credentials provided are invalid.
*   The product is different from the description.
*   The vendor is not responding to queries.

### How to Flow Works:
1.  **Initiate Dispute:**
    *   Go to **My Orders**.
    *   Click on the order that has an issue.
    *   If you used Escrow, click the **"Dispute / Refund"** button.
    *   Fill in the reason for the dispute (e.g., "Invalid Login").
2.  **Dispute Chat:**
    *   A dedicated **Dispute Chat Room** is created between You, the Vendor, and an Admin.
    *   You can upload screenshots (images/videos) as proof in the chat.
3.  **Resolution:**
    *   **Vendor Refund:** The vendor can voluntarily issue a full or partial refund from their dashboard.
    *   **Admin Decision:** If the vendor refuses, an Admin will review the chat and proofs. The Admin has the power to force a refund to the buyer OR release the funds to the vendor if the claim is false.
4.  **Outcome:**
    *   If Won: The funds (Escrow) are returned to your wallet balance or crypto address.
    *   The order status updates to **Refunded**.
